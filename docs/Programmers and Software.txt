AsProgrammer_2.1.2

Software for CH341 Pro Flash Programmer

USE ONLY 25xx socket side, thats the one closer to USB connector,
mind Flash chip notch orientation, notch is the far side from USB connector.

There is only one jumper needed, on the side where the ZIF socket lever is,
PINS "1" and "2" must be connected for 3.3VDC, it is written in chinese on pcb...

MIND MIND MIND:
 BRIDGIN PIN 2 AND 3 is TTL 5VDC and WILL DESTROY THE CHIP,
 >>> best wear protective glasses if pinout is unsure <<<
